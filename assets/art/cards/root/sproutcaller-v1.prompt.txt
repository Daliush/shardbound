ART DIRECTION — SUBSTANTIAL, ATMOSPHERIC FANTASY PAINTING
Paint a tactile, ancient living world with convincing drawing, weight and
organic structure. The main subject dominates the card: for a unit, use
a close character or creature-led composition occupying roughly 80 percent
of the image height; for a spell or relic, give the main action or object
equivalent prominence. Preserve a legible real environment and a clear
silhouette without spending most of the image on surrounding scenery.

Use expressive oil-and-gouache-like paint handling: broad connected planes,
subtle broken color and selected irregular strokes that follow the form.
Material richness comes from the drawing, color and light on substantial
surfaces, with a few refined accents at the focal point. Keep bark, moss,
stone, fabric and living forms tactile when present, but suggest their
texture economically. Treat foliage as overlapping clusters and bark as
large directional planes, not an inventory of leaves and grooves.
Allow contours to soften or disappear in shadow and sharpen at important
lit edges. Brushwork remains visible without turning everything into
graphic streaks, repeated chips or a roughness filter. Preserve the
Oakheart Guardian's organic mass and quiet majestic atmosphere.

Build a palette of forest green, moss, olive, warm bark umber and muted
ochre, with cool grey-green or violet shadows and restrained amber/golden
accents where the scene motivates them. Preserve nuanced natural color
variation. Do not give every subject a glowing heart or uniform green skin.

Lighting is fundamental and specific to the brief. Establish one dominant
motivated light source, selective illumination and believable local bounce.
Use light to reveal volume and material while leaving broad rich colored
shadow masses. A secondary magical light, if requested, affects only its
nearby surroundings. Alternate illumination patterns between cards rather
than always adding a beam from above. Preserve highlight detail and avoid
universal rim glow or evenly illuminated forms.

Create depth with overlap, perspective, relative scale, dark foreground
shapes and softer, less contrasted distant forms. Make the eye find the
subject's expressive feature first. Let the surrounding scene contain
quiet passages and atmospheric breathing room. No photographic blur is
needed to separate planes.

Use the supplied Oakheart Guardian model for paint handling, organic form,

Avoid photorealistic or glossy game rendering, airbrushed surfaces,
