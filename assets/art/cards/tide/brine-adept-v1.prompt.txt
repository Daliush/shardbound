Paint one original Tide-faction illustration for Shardbound. Portrait 2:3.
Card: Brine Adept. Type: unit.
Subject and action: An adult androgynous human tide mage with dark loosely tied hair, a layered teal wrap robe and one small coral brooch, watching a compact water gesture guided by one extended hand. Natural, well-drawn anatomy and a calm intent face..
Setting: A coastal grotto, its dark arch framing a small glimpse of sea and distant rocks. A complete setting suggested in just a few large paint shapes, subordinate to the mage..
Composition: A very close three-quarter portrait cropped at the upper thighs. The mage fills about 85 percent of the image height and nearly all the width. A sleeve and a small sweeping water gesture extend toward the right edge. Face and hand remain large and readable..
Light: Cool light reflected from water at hand height on the right, with weak warm lateral daylight at the distant cave opening. Preserve grouped navy shadows and living earth-toned skin. No overhead beam or all-over glow..

The picture is DRAWN WITH A BRUSH. Let the viewer recognize the painter's
individual gestures. Use a wide worn flat brush loaded with opaque gouache,
plus a few thin dark drawing accents. A single broad sweep describes a
whole major fold or water movement. Show the stroke's beginning, change of
pressure, irregular coverage and imperfect stop. Alternate wet opaque shapes,
thin dragged paint and a few dry bristle skips. Leave quiet underpainting
visible between selected marks. Broad calm dark shapes carry the shadows.

Use FEWER, LARGER marks. Avoid filling those strokes with tiny decorative
chips or drawing a neat outline around every shape. A brushstroke should
construct the form itself, not decorate a fully rendered surface. Vary its
width, direction, edge and ending. Preserve natural drawing and convincing
anatomy with a handful of precise marks at the focal feature. Keep the
remaining description economical and intentionally unresolved in places.

Lively petrol blue, jade, turquoise, deep navy, warm ivory and restrained
coral accents. Matte pigment, subtle warm/cool color relationships, selective
light and a clear value hierarchy. Express water with two or three imperfect
connected strokes, including a dark underside and a sparse cream crest.

The main subject fills the image, with a small but real setting. Keep depth
through overlap and distant values, while reducing backgrounds to a few
quiet painted shapes. Preserve weight, structure and directional lighting.
Use any supplied brush references for their actual shape-making and economy
of gesture, not their characters, colors or isolated backgrounds.

No polished digital rendering, airbrushed faces, regular pointed ribbons,
all-over small marks, granular distress filters, impasto relief, glitter,
spray particles or ornamental clutter. Do not add surface texture as a
substitute for visible brush gestures. No text, frames, logos or signatures.
