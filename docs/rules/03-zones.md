# 3. Zones

*Part of the [Shardbound rulebook](README.md).*

- **3.1** Each player has four zones: their **deck**, their **hand**, their **board** (units and relics) and their **graveyard**.
- **3.2** **Deck**: face down. Nobody knows the order of the cards.
- **3.3** **Hand**: 10 cards at most. A player sees their own hand; their opponent only knows how many cards it holds. If a player draws while their hand is full, the drawn card is discarded.
- **3.4** **Board**: at most 6 units and 3 relics per player. There are no positions; only the order in which cards arrived is kept (used by rule 9.8). If the zone is full, no card can be played into it, and an effect that would put a card there does nothing.
- **3.5** **Graveyard**: public. It holds dead cards, discarded cards and resolved spells.
- **3.6** A token that leaves the board vanishes: it goes neither to the hand nor to the graveyard.
- **3.7** **Information.** Each player knows the opposing faction from the start of the game, but neither the opposing decklist nor hand. Played cards are public: it is up to the players to remember what they saw (see Fracture, 11.2.5).
