# 6. Cards

*Part of the [Shardbound rulebook](README.md).*

- **6.1** Every card has a name, a faction (or Neutral), a type and a cost in Shards.
- **6.2** There are three card types:
  - **Unit**: a max defense, one or two attack abilities (7.1), possibly other abilities and keywords. It stays on the board.
  - **Spell**: an immediate effect. Once resolved, it goes to the graveyard.
  - **Relic**: no defense, only continuous or triggered abilities. It stays on the board. It cannot be attacked; only effects can destroy it or return it to hand.
- **6.3** **Playing a card**: the player pays its cost. A unit or relic arrives on the board and its "Arrival" abilities trigger. A spell applies its effect, then goes to the graveyard.
- **6.4** An ability always takes the form *trigger → effect(s) → target*, built only from the closed lists of sections 8, 9 and 10.
- **6.5** A unit arrives on the board with a current defense equal to its max defense.
- **6.6** A unit whose defense drops to 0 is destroyed immediately, unless it is protected by Anchor or doomed (11.3).
- **6.7** A card returned to hand loses all its modifications and all its damage.
