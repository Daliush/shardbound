# 11.1 Echo X

*Part of the [Shardbound rulebook](README.md). Keyword.*

- **11.1.1** Echo X is a passive carried by an attack ability, not by the unit itself. When the unit dies (9.3), each of its attack abilities that has Echo is replayed, without paying its cost.
- **11.1.2** Every numeric value of the effect is multiplied by X% then rounded down. X can exceed 100: Echo 300 triples the values. Non-numeric effects (Freeze…) apply in full.
- **11.1.3** If the replayed attack has a target, its owner chooses a new one, following the targeting rules of an attack (7.3). With no valid target, those effects do nothing (10.3).
- **11.1.4** An echo is not an attack: it costs nothing and cannot be intercepted.
- **11.1.5** A unit returned to hand does not die: its Echo does not trigger.
- **11.1.6** **Chains**: if an echo kills another unit whose attack ability has Echo, that echo triggers in turn, following the order of rule 9.8.
- **11.1.7** In v1, Echo only exists on attack abilities, so only units have it. If a spell ever gets Echo, it will be replayed immediately after it resolves.
- **11.1.8** If both attack abilities of a unit have Echo, both are replayed, each with its own X, in the order chosen by the owner.
- **11.1.9** Echo replays the attack ability **as printed on the card**. Modifications (8.5) and auras (8.14) that applied to the unit are lost when it dies, so they do not boost the echo.

> *Example*: a unit dies; its attack ability "Freeze + 10 damage" has Echo 50. The echo applies "Freeze + 5 damage" to a new target. If the unit's other attack ability has no Echo, it is not replayed.
