# 7. Combat

*Part of the [Shardbound rulebook](README.md).*

- **7.1** Each unit has one or two attack abilities. Each one has its own cost in Shards and its own effect (damage, freeze…). Most attack abilities have a single target; some have none (7.8).
- **7.2** A unit can attack if all of the following hold:
  - it did not arrive on the board this turn;
  - it is not frozen;
  - it has not already attacked this turn (a unit with two attack abilities uses only one of them per turn);
  - its controller can pay the cost of the attack ability used.
- **7.3** **Attack target**: an enemy unit. The opposing player can only be targeted if they have no unit on the board. Relics cannot be attacked.
- **7.4** **Attack sequence**:
  1. the active player chooses the attacking unit, one of its attack abilities and its target (if it has one), then pays that ability's cost;
  2. the attacking unit's "Attack" abilities trigger (9.9);
  3. if the target is a unit, the defender may intercept (7.5);
  4. the effect of the attack ability applies to the target.
- **7.5** **Intercept**: when an attack targets one of their units, the defender may redirect the attack to another of their units, which becomes the new target.
  - A unit can intercept at most once per turn.
  - A frozen unit cannot intercept.
  - Neither a spell nor an echo can be intercepted (11.1.4).
- **7.6** **No retaliation**: the target suffers the effect and deals nothing back.
- **7.7** Spells are not attacks: a spell can target the opposing player even if they have units, if the card allows it.
- **7.8** Besides, or instead of, hitting a target, an attack ability may affect the attacking unit itself, its controller, or all of its controller's units. Examples: "Deal 4 damage to the target. Draw 1 card." or "Give all your units +5/+0 until end of turn." An attack that only affects its own side has no target: it cannot be intercepted (7.5). It still counts as the unit's attack for the turn (7.2) and still triggers "Attack" abilities (9.9).
