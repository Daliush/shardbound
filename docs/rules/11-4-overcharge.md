# 11.4 Overcharge

*Part of the [Shardbound rulebook](README.md). Keyword.*

- **11.4.1** When playing a card with Overcharge, the player may choose to overcharge it: it then costs 2 Shards less, never below 0.
- **11.4.2** On that player's next turn, 2 of their Shards are locked: they are not refilled (4.2). Their max Shards do not change.
- **11.4.3** Overcharges stack: each overcharged card locks 2 Shards.
- **11.4.4** If the locked Shards exceed the Shards to refill, the player has 0 Shards that turn. The excess is lost, not carried over.
- **11.4.5** Overcharge only reduces the cost of playing the card, never the cost of an attack ability.

> *Example*: on turn 5, with 5 Shards, I play a 4-cost card and overcharge it: it costs me 2. On turn 6, my max goes up to 6, but only 4 Shards are usable.
