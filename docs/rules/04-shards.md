# 4. Shards

*Part of the [Shardbound rulebook](README.md).*

- **4.1** Each player starts the game with a maximum of 0 Shards. At the start of each of their turns, this maximum increases by 1, up to 10.
- **4.2** The player then refills their Shards up to their maximum, minus the Shards locked by Overcharge (11.4).
- **4.3** Shards pay for playing cards and for attack abilities.
- **4.4** Unspent Shards are lost at the end of the turn.
