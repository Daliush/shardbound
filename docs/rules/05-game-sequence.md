# 5. Game sequence

*Part of the [Shardbound rulebook](README.md).*

## 5.1 Setup

- **5.1.1** The first player is chosen at random.
- **5.1.2** Each player shuffles their deck and draws 5 cards.
- **5.1.3** **Mulligan**: each player may, once, shuffle their whole hand back into their deck, then draw one card fewer (4 cards).

## 5.2 Start of turn (in this order)

1. **5.2.1** Anchor protections on the active player's units end (11.3).
2. **5.2.2** The active player gains and refills their Shards (4.1, 4.2).
3. **5.2.3** They draw a card. The first player does not draw on their very first turn.
4. **5.2.4** Their "Turn start" abilities trigger.

## 5.3 Main phase

- **5.3.1** The active player may, in any order and as long as their Shards allow: play a card from their hand (6.3) or attack with a unit (7).
- **5.3.2** They end their turn whenever they decide to.

## 5.4 End of turn (in this order)

1. **5.4.1** The active player's "Turn end" abilities trigger.
2. **5.4.2** "Until end of turn" effects end.
3. **5.4.3** The active player's doomed units (11.3.4) whose defense is still 0 are destroyed.
4. **5.4.4** Unspent Shards are lost.

## 5.5 During the opponent's turn

- **5.5.1** A player can never play a card during the opponent's turn.
- **5.5.2** Their only possible action is to intercept (7.5).
